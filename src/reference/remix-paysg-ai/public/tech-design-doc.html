Executive Summary & Problem Statement

Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) represent over 99% of all enterprises in Singapore. Due to lean operating structures, SMEs typically have 1 or 2 Accounts Payable (AP) personnel (and frequently the business owner or office manager handles bookkeeping directly).

AP team members are inundated daily by vendor correspondence arriving via Gmail inboxes and physical delivery slips. These documents span varied formats: Tax Invoices, Statements of Account (SOA), Delivery Orders (DO), Goods Receipts (GRN), and Credit Notes.

⚠️ Critical Pain Points
  • Manual entry fatigue re-typing vendor UEN, invoice numbers, line items, and bank details into Xero.
  • IRAS 9% GST compliance risk (claiming input tax from non-registered suppliers or SOAs).
  • Procurement overcharges (paying invoices without cross-checking PO rates or signed delivery orders).
  • Manual payment execution through corporate banking portals risking typographical errors.
💡 PaySG AI Solution
  • Dual ingestion: Connects to AP Gmail inbox via OAuth 2.0 + Drag-and-drop manual portal.
  • Multimodal Gemini 3.8 Flash OCR extracts UEN, GST, and line items in <1.5 seconds.
  • Automated 3-Way Match reconciles PO, DO, and Invoice before money leaves the bank.
  • 1-click sync to Xero Accounting (ACCPAY bills) and SGQR PayNow instant transfer.

AI Engineering & Gemini 3.8 Flash Implementation

The prototype bypasses traditional two-step OCR (e.g., rasterizing image to noisy text then passing to an LLM). Instead, Gemini 3.8 Flash natively reads PDF and image bytes directly through multimodal tensors, preserving bounding boxes, spatial alignment, and tabular hierarchies.

Multimodal Prompt Architecture

POST /api/ai/classify-and-extract
Payload: { textContent?: string, base64Data: string, mimeType: "application/pdf", fileName: string }

The model receives the binary buffer paired with strict schema enforcement:
  • Classifies into 9 discrete document types (Tax Invoice, Proforma, SOA, DO, GRN, etc.)
  • Extracts Singapore UEN, IRAS GST Number, and PayNow Corporate UEN proxy
  • Performs mathematical verification: Subtotal + 9% GST == Total Amount (±$0.02)
  • Suggests accounting GL code: 400 (COGS), 429 (General), 445 (IT), 493 (Logistics)

The 3-Way Match Engine: Rules & PO/GR Requirements

❓ Do SME Owners Have to Upload PO or GRN?

NO. Uploading PO and Delivery Orders is completely flexible and NOT required for every invoice:

1. Recurring / Non-PO

Telco (Singtel), Utilities (SP Group), and AWS subscriptions are tagged as "Exempt / Non-PO". Post directly to Xero with 1 click.

2. Auto-Match Invoices

If the supplier prints your PO# (e.g. PO-2026-089) on the bill, Gemini automatically matches it against existing orders.

3. Active Procurement

When PO/DO are provided, AI checks line-by-line item counts, catching price inflation and undelivered items before payment.

Document Primary Question Answered Protection Provided to SME
1. Purchase Order (PO) "What was approved to buy and at what contracted unit price?" Prevents unauthorized procurement & supplier price markups.
2. Delivery Order (DO/GRN) "What was physically signed and received by our warehouse/office?" Prevents paying for missing, damaged, or short-delivered goods.
3. Vendor Tax Invoice "What is the supplier billing us for (inclusive of 9% GST)?" Validates math, checks IRAS compliance, and generates PayNow QR.

Security, Data Privacy & OAuth 2.0 Boundaries

🔒 Least-Privilege Gmail Access

The OAuth flow strictly requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly. The app has zero permission to send, delete, or modify user emails.

🛡️ Zero Persistent Credential Storage

OAuth tokens are held in volatile client memory (cachedAccessToken) and transmitted via HTTPS Bearer headers. No email tokens or user passwords are saved on unencrypted server disks.

Singapore Accounting Localizations (IRAS & PayNow)

IRAS Form 5 Box 7 (Input Tax Claims)

Under IRAS regulations, input tax can only be claimed if a valid Tax Invoice is issued by a GST-registered business. PaySG AI automatically differentiates claimable invoices from non-claimable documents (e.g. Statements of Account, which are legally barred from GST claims).

SGQR PayNow Corporate Disbursal

Gemini extracts the vendor's 9 or 10-digit Singapore Unique Entity Number (UEN). The app generates a real-time SGQR PayNow preview containing the exact invoice balance, eliminating manual typing errors on bank portals.

Scenario 1: 100% 3-Way Match (OfficeWorks SG) Full Match

Scenario 2: Price Discrepancy Detection (OfficeWorks Desks) Price Variance

PO contracted at $660.00/unit, but supplier billed at $700.00/unit. AI flags a +$218.00 variance and holds automated payment until manager sign-off.

Scenario 3: Short Delivery & Credit Note Rebate (Sheng Siong) Shortage

Invoice billed 5 coffee packs, but warehouse signed for 3. AI flags the missing 2 cartons (-$130.80) and pairs with Credit Note SS-CN-088 to balance the ledger.

Scenario 4: Non-PO Recurring Telco (Singtel Fibre) Exempt Non-PO

Recurring utility bill ($926.50). AI validates GST M2-0000000-8, verifies math, flags PO matching as Exempt, and enables instant Xero posting.
