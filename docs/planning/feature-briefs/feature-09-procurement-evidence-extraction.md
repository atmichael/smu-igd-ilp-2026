# Procurement Evidence Extraction

**Priority:** Should-have

As an accounts-payable user, I want purchase orders and receipt or service-acceptance documents converted into reviewable structured evidence so that invoice amounts can be checked against what was authorized and received.

Extract ordered items, quantities, prices, and purchase-order references from buyer purchase orders or service orders. Extract received quantities from goods receipts or delivery evidence, and accepted quantities or milestones from service-acceptance records. Preserve source traceability and identify missing or uncertain values for review. A seller-side sales order must not be substituted for a buyer purchase order.

Clarify supported fields and how partial deliveries, service milestones, amendments, and documents without a purchase-order reference are represented. Do not create payable invoice lines, perform matching, or approve payment in this feature.
