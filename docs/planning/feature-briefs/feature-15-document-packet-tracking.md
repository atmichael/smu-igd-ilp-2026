# AP Document Packet Tracking

**Priority:** Should-have

As an accounts-payable user, I want to track each invoice case and its related documents so that I can see what has arrived, what is still processing, and which expected evidence is pending even when documents arrive at different times or through different channels.

Create or link a case when the first document arrives, using agreed business references such as supplier and purchase-order number. Track each document through arrival, processing, review, and verification. Show the received documents, their source and timestamps, the expected documents, and the case's readiness for matching. Support later arrivals from mailbox, upload, or camera and make duplicates, unlinked documents, and ambiguous links visible for review.

Mark a document as pending only when a purchase order, service order, or configured business rule establishes that it is expected. Do not infer that a document is missing from inbox silence alone. Clarify case-matching keys, required evidence by transaction type, partial receipt handling, and how users resolve ambiguous or duplicate documents. Do not perform three-way matching or payment approval in this feature.
